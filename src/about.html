About

Hi there! I really think it is awesome you are visiting my website. My name is Ruben and I am a game (engine) developer and a professional full-stack .NET developer.

What started as a hobby quickly became the origin story of my career as software developer. While I love creating games and libraries for games it is hard to find a job doing exactly that. More often than not it is encouraged to start your own indie studio. I personally found that a little bit to much of a gamble and I am not the only one. Like myself there are many game developers who do game dev in their spare time and do something else as a job.

I was lucky to be able to use the knowledge I gained as game developer to find my way in the world of software and web development. Despite games being very visual and requiring a different mindset, technically they have a lot in common with business applicationse. Therefore the knowledge I gain as full-stack developer can be used for my personal projects and visa versa. This means I am constantly learning new stuff while having a lot of fun doing so. :D
